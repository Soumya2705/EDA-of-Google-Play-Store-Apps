08 / process

Data cleaning & methodology

Raw scraped Play Store data needs work before it's analysis-ready. Here's what the pipeline handled.

  • 01Reviews — cast to numeric, expanding "M" suffixes (e.g. 3.2M → 3,200,000).
  • 02Size — normalized "M"/"k" suffixes to a single KB unit; dropped "Varies with device".
  • 03Installs — stripped "+" and commas, cast to integer for true numeric aggregation.
  • 04Price — stripped "$" and cast to float to compute revenue potential.
  • 05Last Updated — parsed to datetime; derived day / month / year fields for trend analysis.
  • 06Android Ver — extracted the leading version number via regex from mixed strings ("4.0 and up", "Varies with device").
  • 07Duplicates — removed duplicate rows and repeated app entries, keeping the first record.
  • 08Result — analysis-ready rows across categories.
